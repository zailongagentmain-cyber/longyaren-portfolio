The short version

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics

    This site is a set of static HTML pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking script, embeds no third-party content, and loads no external fonts, CDNs or social widgets. There is no profile of you stored anywhere, because there is no mechanism on this site that would build one.

    The practical consequence is that reading these articles leaves no cookie behind, and no third party receives a notification that you were here.

    TRUST

What the site does and does not run

  • An inspectable page, end to end

    Every page is plain HTML with its own stylesheet inlined. There is no JavaScript on this site, no embedded video player, no chat widget, no advertising tag and no embedded form. If a page looks simple, it is simple in the markup as well as in the browser.

    The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, the hosting infrastructure may keep standard server request logs, and the hosting provider's own privacy policy governs what it does with them. This site adds no tracking on top of that, and the author cannot read or use those logs.

    TECH

  • Outbound links and mail links

    Articles link to external sources, which is what makes the sourcing checkable. Those sites are operated by other people and have their own privacy practices, which this site does not control and does not speak for. Following an external link is the moment you leave this site and its practices.

    Email links open your own mail application with the address filled in. Nothing is transmitted, and nothing is logged by this site, until you press send in your own client.

Email and Upwork

  • How the email address is used

    The address published on this site exists for one purpose: to answer enquiries and corrections sent to it. It is not sold, rented, shared or traded, and it is never added to a mailing list. There is no newsletter and no subscription of any kind.

    An email that arrives in that inbox is read by the author and answered. If a message contains details that would identify a client or a third party, ask before it is quoted or referenced anywhere.

    SEO

  • Work conducted through Upwork

    Engagements are contracted and paid through Upwork, so the personal details required for contracting, invoicing and platform compliance are handled under Upwork's own privacy policy rather than this one. That policy, not this page, governs the data exchanged there.

Children and sensitive information

  • Audience and data

    This site is written for a professional audience and is not directed at children. Please do not send personal data that is sensitive by nature, such as government identifiers, payment card details or health records, through the email address. Nothing on this site asks for such information, and such a message would be deleted unread rather than stored.
