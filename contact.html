There is no contact form, on purpose

  • Why there is no form

    This site is a static build with no server-side code and no form backend, so a contact form here would either do nothing or hand the message to a third-party service. Rather than ship a form that silently fails, there are two working channels: the Upwork message thread and a plain mail link.

    Clicking the email link opens your own mail application with the address already filled in. Nothing is transmitted until you press send, and nothing is stored by this site either way.

What to expect after you write

  • Reply times

    The expectations below are commitments rather than aspirations, and they are the same for every channel.

    • Upwork messages: a reply within 1-2 business days, and up to 3 business days if a message lands over a weekend or a public holiday.
    • Email: a reply within 2-3 business days.
    • Corrections to published articles: acknowledged within 5 business days. A substantive data dispute gets a written reply naming the conflicting sources rather than a bare agreement.
    • Audit and consulting quotes: a scoped proposal with a fixed price and timeline, normally within 3 business days of receiving enough context.
